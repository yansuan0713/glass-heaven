# GLASS HEAVEN
### Cyberpunk 2077 Expansion Concept
## English Dialogue — Portfolio Edition
*Release Candidate 1*
---
**Context.** GLASS HEAVEN is a narrative expansion for Cyberpunk 2077 set aboard the Crystal Palace, a luxury orbital station. V arrives as a patient: the Relic is overwriting the neural architecture that makes V a person, and orbital medicine offers a treatment that acts on the structures responsible for identity rather than merely postponing failure. What follows is the expansion's English dialogue — three missions, four route endings, six principal voices. Lines are grouped by mission and scene, with the minimum context needed to read them. Player-selected lines and route-specific sequences are marked where the distinction matters.
**A note on how to read this.** Every line in this document is the finished English script. Record identifiers appear as bracketed tags for navigation only. Where a scene contains a deliberate silence, it is marked as such and is not a missing line.
---

## Scene · The Treatment Bargain
**Context.** V confronts the man who has been offering a way out. He has never claimed the treatment will save anyone, and he does not start now. The more thoroughly the player investigated beforehand, the harder he is to mislead.

`[GH-045]`

`[GH-046]`
**Mr. Blue Eyes.** Give you more options.
