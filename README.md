# GLASS HEAVEN
## Cyberpunk 2077 Expansion Concept
**Narrative Design & English Localization Portfolio**

> **Spoiler note.** This package discusses *Cyberpunk 2077* plot points and endings, including The Sun ending. Recommended for readers who have finished the base game and *Phantom Liberty*.
---
## The Pitch
The Crystal Palace is a luxury orbital station where the wealthy buy the one thing Night City cannot sell them: the right to remain themselves. V arrives as a patient. The Relic is overwriting the neural architecture that makes V a person, and orbital medicine offers a treatment that acts on the structures responsible for identity rather than merely postponing failure. Surviving it means becoming someone whose continuity with the person who consented can no longer be taken for granted.
GLASS HEAVEN is built around one interactive question: **if treatment changes the parts of you that decide what matters, at what point does the person who wakes up stop being the person who agreed to it?** The missions, the four endings and the systems are each a different answer to that question — and the player reaches an ending by acting, not by choosing from a menu.
---
## What This Portfolio Demonstrates
**Branching narrative design.** Four endings, each reached through play rather than a prompt, with route-specific scenes, conditional dialogue and variant sequences written end to end.
**Behavioural route differentiation.** The routes differ in what the player *does* — information withheld or shared, alliances kept or burned, people left behind, compromises accepted — not in tone or cosmetic outcome.
**Dialogue writing.** A locked English dialogue corpus of 95 records across six principal voices, written to be performed rather than read, including two deliberately silent beats.
**Character voice.** Six distinct registers sustained across three missions, with a documented voice principle per character and no reliance on catchphrases or stereotype.
**Localization and English adaptation.** The script is an adaptation, not a translation: dramatic intent and character voice take precedence over word-for-word structure, and intentional ambiguity is preserved.
**Reactive narrative systems.** Conditional dialogue, consequence tracking and route reactivity — how the narrative notices meaningful player actions and reflects them later, without scoring them.

## The Four Routes
**BLUE** — V transfers the system off-station intact, in exchange for a treatment protocol, and watches the records leave by name.
**AURELIA** — V separates the medical model from the prediction core, destroys the core, and decides case by case which data to erase permanently — accepting that every erasure costs some patient a treatment that currently works.
**OPEN SKY** — V publishes the proof of what was done while destroying the ability to do it again, and learns that evidence changes institutions less than anyone hopes.
**ASHES** — V destroys the project entirely: no cure, no evidence, no reform, and nothing kept.
Each route costs something the others do not, and leaves a different question open. **None is ranked, and none is written as a warning.**

## Scope
This is a **portfolio-grade narrative design concept**: a design brief, a dialogue sample, and the documentation to read them.
It is **not** an implemented REDengine mod, not a playable build, and not a production specification. Quest implementation, state architecture, save behaviour and engine serialisation sit outside its scope and appear only where narrative comprehension requires them.
